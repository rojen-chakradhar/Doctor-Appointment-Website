Privacy Policy


We may collect information about you directly from you and from third parties, as well as automatically through your use of our Site or Services.


Information We Collect Directly From You.

Users may browse the Site without registering to our system. However, registration is required to use the Services. Once a Healthcare Provider has registered with us and subscribed to our Services, the Healthcare Provider can then invite Patients to use the Services provided by La Pulga. After receiving an invite, the Patient would then need to register for a La Pulga account to use the Services.


Healthcare Practitioner’s Registration. To register, Healthcare practitioners must provide certain information about themselves and their practices including the Healthcare practitioner’s name, date of birth, gender, and other important information; the Healthcare practitioner’s practice name, address, phone number, and a user id/password. We may also collect certain optional information, including middle name or initial, bank account or other payment information, and other contact details, pictures, and communication preferences.


Patient Registration. Patients must provide certain information, including name, date of birth, gender, address, email, mobile, and work phone, as well as a user id and password. We may also collect certain optional information, including middle name or initial, home phone and other contact info, pharmacy name, and contact details, In addition, the Patient can communicate other health-related information to the Healthcare Provider during a video consultation via the La Pulga website.

SECURITY OF MY PERSONAL INFORMATION

All are in client side. So, nobody except you can access your personal informations.

Access To My Personal Information

You may modify the registration information that you have submitted by logging into your account and updating your profile information.

CONTACT US

We encourage you to contact us if you have any questions or concerns regarding the privacy aspects of our Services or would like to make a complaint. You may contact us at La Pulga in which the Helpline number is provided on the footer of website.
